A little clarity

About your support

Which projects does my contribution support?

Choose a project to cheer for; its name is shown in the PayPal payment item. This is shared support for all Ev-Cabinet apps and projects. Contributions help fund development, maintenance, testing, and running costs.

Will I be charged every month?

No. This is a one-time contribution, not an automatically renewing subscription. Enter your preferred USD amount on PayPal and review it before paying.

Does contributing unlock features or priority support?

No extra features or priority support are included. Your contribution is a voluntary way to support Ev-Cabinet's development and maintenance.

Will my name or payment details be published?

This website does not publish supporter names or payment records. If we would like to include you in a future thank-you message, we will ask for your permission first.
