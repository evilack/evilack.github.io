QuietMic

A lightweight virtual microphone for Windows that reduces keyboard sounds and background noise in real time. Your audio stays on your PC and is never recorded.

  • 01 Real-time noise suppression powered by RNNoise
  • 02 High-pass filtering to reduce low-frequency keyboard vibrations
  • 03 Presets for natural speech and quieter environments
  • 04 Eight languages, including Korean
